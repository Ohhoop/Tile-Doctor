[h2]What it is[/h2]
Tile Doctor is a development and debugging tool. It lets you look at what is really sitting on the tiles around your character, and fix the ones that are broken: invisible collisions, leftovers from removed/bugged mods, objects that cannot be picked up or used anymore, etc.

[h2]How it works[/h2]
Right-click anywhere and choose Tile Doctor. A window opens with a table of everything on the 9 tiles around your character, and a yellow outline shows the scanned area in the world. The table follows you as you move.

For each object, corpse and vehicle it shows:
- The tile it stands on
- Its type and name
- Its tile, or its item type for items on the ground
- Its collision and structure properties
- A red alert symbol when something looks broken (missing tile, tile from a removed mod, invisible object). Detection is not perfect: a flagged object might be fine and a broken one might be missed, it is only there to help you find the issue faster.

Hover a row to outline that object in the world. Be careful: an outline can be hidden when a wall or something else blocks the view between you and the object.

Possible actions on each row:
- Delete: removes that single object, corpse, item or vehicle. A deleted floor is replaced by dirt, a wooden floor upstairs, or the same water tile, so you never end up with a hole.
- Reset: rebuilds the object from scratch, as if freshly placed. Its tile and finishes (wallpaper, paint, trim) are kept, its state goes back to neutral: a stove turns off, a door closes, a container comes back empty.

[h2]Important[/h2]
This is a debugging tool: deleting or resetting objects cannot be undone. Use it knowingly, and keep a backup of your save if you are unsure.

I was obviously not able to test every single scenario: there is a ridiculous amount of tiles, objects and items in this game, and that is without even talking about mods. If something does not behave as expected, a reset that goes wrong, an object that should be flagged and is not, or anything else, please let me know in the comment section and I will do my best to handle it.

[h2]Q&A[/h2]
Q: Is it safe to add or remove mid-save?
A: Yes. Tile Doctor saves nothing in your world: it is designed to be added, fix the corrupted tile and be removed.

Q: Does it work in multiplayer?
A: Yes. In single player anyone can use it. In multiplayer it is reserved for admins, and every action is checked by the server.

Q: Can it restore a tile to how the map originally looked?
A: No. The original map data is only readable by the game engine. Tile Doctor works on what is currently on the tiles.

Q: I found a case it does not handle, what should I do?
A: Post it in the comment section with the "Type" and "Tile / item" columns of the row in question, the mod you think it comes from if applicable and, if possible, the error from your console.txt (in C:\Users\YourName\Zomboid).
